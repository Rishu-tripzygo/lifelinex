Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a diagnosis?

The online assessment itself isn't a diagnosis — it just gives your doctor context before your call. The consultation is a real medical consultation: your doctor can diagnose your condition and prescribe medication, supplements or a personalised treatment plan, and will advise if in-person testing or a specialist referral is needed.

What happens on the call?

Your doctor reviews your assessment answers and asks follow-up questions about your history, symptoms and goals, then explains what a personalised plan would involve for you.

Is the consultation fee refundable?

The consultation fee is non-refundable. If you choose to go ahead with your personalised plan, the full fee is adjusted against it — no extra charge.

How soon do I get my plan after the call?

Most plans are delivered within 2–4 hours of your consultation.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your assessment answers and consultation details are used only to build your plan and are not shared outside our clinical team.
