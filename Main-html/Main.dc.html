What happens after your consultation

Your doctor reviews your assessment and consultation, then lets you know what would genuinely help. Depending on your situation, that might include:

Recommended diagnostic tests

A structured health protocol

Educational guidance for your specific concern

Relevant products, supplements or ongoing support, where appropriate

Recommendations vary from person to person, and you decide whether to go ahead with any additional paid offering — nothing is added automatically.

Doctor-Led, Every Time

Frequently Asked Questions

What is LifelineX?

LifelineX is a doctor-led online platform that helps you understand a health concern, consult a qualified doctor, and decide on an appropriate next step.

How does the online consultation work?

After your assessment, you pay the consultation fee and book a time online. You'll then be connected with a qualified doctor for your consultation.

What is included in the ₹999 consultation?

The ₹999 fee covers your assessment review and a 1-on-1 consultation with a qualified doctor.

Are diagnostic tests included in ₹999?

No. If your doctor recommends diagnostic tests, these are arranged and charged separately.

Are protocols or products included in the consultation price?

No. Any recommended protocol, product or supplement is offered separately, and only if relevant to you.

How is a doctor assigned?

Doctors are assigned based on availability and the health concern you've chosen. All LifelineX doctors are qualified (MBBS/MD).

Can I choose not to purchase a protocol?

Yes. The consultation gives you your doctor's recommendations — deciding whether to proceed with anything further is entirely up to you.

How will I receive my consultation recommendations?

Your doctor shares recommendations with you directly after your consultation, along with any suggested next steps.

Is LifelineX suitable for emergencies?

No. LifelineX is not an emergency service.

What should I do if I need urgent medical attention?

Please contact your local emergency services or visit the nearest hospital immediately. Do not wait for an online consultation.
